# Third-party material and scientific references

## Source-derived data and audit images

Source: Vaibhav A. Edlabadkar, Rushi U. Soni, A. B. M. Shaheen ud Doulah,
Stephen Y. Owusu, Samuel Hackett, Joshua M. Bartels, Nicholas Leventis and
Chariklia Sotiriou-Leventis. CO2 Uptake by Microporous Carbon Aerogels Derived
from Polybenzoxazine and Analogous All-Nitrogen Polybenzodiazine Aerogels.
Chemistry of Materials 36(3), 1172-1187 (2024).

- Main article: https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemmater.3c01717
- Supporting Information: https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemmater.3c01717.s001
- Metadata: https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/25019778
- Supporting-record license: CC BY-NC 4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
- Source page and panel: S-5, Table S.2, C-PBDAZ-300.

The files in `data/published/`, the digitization overlay and published-data
figures are attributed adaptations of that supporting record. Their source
CC BY-NC designation is retained. Changes include automatic coordinate
selection, affine axis calibration, thinning of merged red-marker regions,
refitting and analytical annotations. Original authors do not endorse this
project. The original PDF is not included.

## New implementation and synthetic materials

The Python implementation, coding question, independently generated synthetic
CSV files, task answers, tests and synthetic figures were created for this
portfolio with OpenAI assistance. They use the MIT project license, separately
from source-derived materials. The candidate-only archive contains these
independent materials and no source-derived plot data.
